About GRT

Corporate Sales

Every commemoration, celebration, and milestone deserves a meaningful memento, and at GRT, we know all too well about the journey a brand makes from its inception to its recognition. Through our exclusive, diverse, and customizable corporate gifting options, we help you celebrate these invaluable memories with exquisite ornaments and gifting articles.

GRT-The promise of more

The name of GRT has been showered by the love and adoration of our customers since well over our golden jubilee. Drawing inspiration from your perpetual patronage and our love for creativity, GRT has ventured into Hospitality, launching a chain of premium stays that embody every aspect of luxurious relaxation and rejuvenation. 16 exclusive locations in the form of GRT Hotels and Resorts, scattered across the choicest destinations over South India, are now accessible to the people for a regenerative getaway. Laced with abundant modern amenities, a hospitable, trained staff, facilities for relaxation and unwinding is a staple at all our upscale hospitality establishments.

GRT Offerings

To continually delight our customers, GRT presents a wide array of exquisite collections in gold, silver, platinum, and diamond. Our designs draw inspiration from the timeless traditional craft of jewellery making, enhanced by the creativity of our skilled artisans and designers.

Awards & Accolades

Awards and accolades have been an important part of the GRT Jewellers success story. The company has bagged several recognitions at the Indian and global levels.

Our Story

In the bustling streets of 1964 Madras, the visionary Shri G. Rajendran founded GRT Jewellers, planting the seeds for what would become one of the most celebrated and leading jewellery brands in India's jewellery landscape.

CSR Activities

True growth occurs when we grow together as a community. Our dedication to our customers and the community expands beyond the boundaries of our business. The aftershocks of the second wave of Covid-19 left the masses struggling to survive. GRT donated generously to relief efforts through contributions to the CM Public Relief Fund to help in any we could.

GRT Education

The GRT Group of Educational Institutions was founded with the vision of becoming a leader in providing modern and pragmatic education. In response to the evolving needs of a technologically driven younger generation, our institutions blend cutting-edge technology with visionary ideals to prepare students for a vast array of opportunities and help them excel in their chosen fields.
